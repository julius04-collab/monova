FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Monova? +

A personal finance planner that turns your income, spending, budgets and goals into one clear monthly view.

Is Monova a bank? +

No. It doesn't hold, move or invest money, and it doesn't give financial advice.

Is Monova free to use? +

Yes. Free covers an overview, one budget and one goal with no time limit.

Can I create multiple savings goals? +

Free includes one goal. Plus allows as many as you like.

Does Monova connect to my bank account? +

Not in this concept. Bank connections aren't implemented; you add your numbers yourself.

Can I cancel my paid plan? +

Yes, anytime from settings. You keep Plus until the period ends, then move to Free with your data intact.

How does Monova handle personal information? +

The intended approach: collect only what you enter, explain it plainly, never sell it, and let you export or delete it.
